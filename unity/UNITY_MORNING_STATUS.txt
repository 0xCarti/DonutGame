CURRENT SCOPE
One fairground venue with Opening Day, Practice, and earned After Hours.
Physical donuts travel up the incline, fall into the tray, and become waste if
spilled. Keep the scoop while collecting packaging; scoop, pour, coat, serve.
Independent animated fryer dials, repair/refill, overheating/fire/extinguishing,
rapid tray-shake chaos, a service bell and instant customer-skipping punches.
Original authored FBX props and skinned characters, articulated hand contact,
folded paper bags, curved sugar labels, detailed material maps, reflections,
shadows, fairground ticket UI, warnings, end receipt, sound and score feedback.

cosmetics, account services and additional venues remain version-2 work.
