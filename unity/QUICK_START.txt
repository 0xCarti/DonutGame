GOLDEN HOUR 1.1.0 — UNITY WINDOWS EDITION

GET STARTED

1. Extract the entire ZIP to a folder on your Windows PC.
2. Open that folder and run GoldenHour.exe. Keep GoldenHour_Data,
   UnityPlayer.dll, and all other supplied runtime files beside the EXE.
   You do not need the Unity editor.
3. Choose PRACTICE AT THE FAIR to learn without a clock or sales quota.
   The FIELD GUIDE explains the counter. Settings includes sound, music,
   and Reduced Motion for instant workstation position changes.

MAKE AN ORDER

Read the customer's ticket: a bag holds 6 donuts; a bucket holds 12.
Wait for each hand/camera movement to finish before the next action.

  1 or 2    Choose a bag or bucket.
  R         Pick up the scoop; press R again to scoop 3 donuts from the tray.
  Q         Pour the loaded scoop into your package.
  R, Q      Repeat until the package is full (2 loads for a bag, 4 for a bucket).
  C or V    Add cinnamon sugar or powdered sugar to match the ticket.
  Q         With an empty scoop, lift the prepared package from the mat.
  E         Hand the finished order to the customer.

You can also click the physical stations and tools. Q pours while the scoop
is loaded; otherwise it lifts or sets down your package. Serving a correctly
sized, correctly coated order earns money and builds your service streak.
Using the customer action empty-handed skips them and breaks the streak.

COUNTER CONTROLS

  Tab       Toggle the whole-counter view and the working view.
  1 / 2     Paper bag / sharing bucket.
  R         Equip the scoop, then scoop from the tray.
  Q         Pour, or lift/set down the package when the scoop is empty.
  C / V     Cinnamon sugar / powdered sugar.
  E         Serve the prepared order.
  F         Use the extinguisher when the fryer catches fire.
  F1        Open or close the Field Guide.
  F11       Toggle fullscreen.
  Esc       Pause or resume.

Use Tab to see the machine controls. Left-click a heat/feed dial to lower it;
right-click to raise it. Hold the motor service control to clear a jam, or
hold the hopper to refill batter. After a fire, let the fryer settle and
restore the heat. Fresh donuts count as stock after they land in the tray.
Rapid tray clicks can spill donuts. Use the discard opening for a wrong order;
plain donuts return to stock when there is room.

MODES

Opening Day is one timed shift with a sales target. Relaxed Pace is available
on the menu. Clear Opening Day to earn After Hours, the continuing service
mode. Practice has no clock or quota and is the place to learn the controls.

This is the Unity Windows edition. The older Godot campaign is a separate
edition; its campaign content and progression are not this release's modes.
