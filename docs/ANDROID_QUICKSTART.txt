GOLDEN HOUR - ANDROID 1.2.0

INSTALL
Download GoldenHour-1.2.0-Android.apk onto your Android device and open it.
If prompted, allow your browser or file app to install this APK, then choose
Install. Open Golden Hour. No store account or internet connection is needed
to play. Android 7.0 or later is required; phone performance still needs testing.

FIRST PLAY
Hold your device sideways and choose PRACTICE to learn without a timer.
Read the front customer's ticket. Tap a bag or bucket, pick up the scoop,
tap the fresh tray, then tap the container to pour. Repeat until full.
Tap the requested sugar, lift the finished order, then tap the customer.

Drag the counter to look around. Center resets the camera.
Hold the jam control or batter hopper to repair or refill. Release to stop.
Tools provides larger buttons, and the gold button suggests your next action.
Watch the heat and fault messages. Pause or Android Back pauses the game.

GRAPHICS AND FEEDBACK
Start with Balanced in Settings. Choose Low if the phone struggles.
Settings shows the phone model and graphics details for troubleshooting.
Please report the phone model, Android version, and what happened if you find
an issue. A screenshot or short recording is useful.

PROGRESS
Campaign progress saves on this device. Practice preserves your campaign.
Install matching signed updates over the existing app to keep your progress.
Uninstalling removes local saves. The Ticket Booth uses earned Tickets. Real-money checkout is unavailable; no ads.

NEW IN 1.2
Appeal affects arrivals. Wrong full/coated orders sell for less and hurt appeal.
Tools > Fryer adjusts feed speed independently of heat.
Your Collection > Ticket Booth offers cosmetics and one-shift supplies.
Practice > Pause > Explore Venues & Machines previews all three locations.
