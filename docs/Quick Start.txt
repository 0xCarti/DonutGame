GOLDEN HOUR - MINI DONUT CLUB
Playtest 0.1.0

OPEN YOUR STAND

On Windows, open GoldenHour.exe and choose OPEN THE STAND. No account or
internet connection is required. Keep the LICENSES folder with the game.

For the web build, host this folder over HTTP or HTTPS and open index.html
through that server. Opening the HTML directly from disk will not work.
WebGL 2 and WebAssembly are required. A desktop Chromium browser or Firefox
is the initial playtest target. Click once to allow browser audio playback.

This is an early playable build. It has one fairground, six escalating shifts,
and twenty upgrades. Steam integration, controller support, native phone
packages, and additional venues are not included in this version.

MAKE THE QUOTA

1. Choose cinnamon, powdered sugar, or cocoa.
2. Choose destination tray 1, 2, or 3, then drop six donuts into the hopper.
3. Watch them fry, flip, and land. Several batches can cook at once.
4. Sell six matching donuts for a base 2x payout. Full mixed trays start at
   1.6x. Partial trays also sell, but usually earn less.
5. Meet the sales target before closing. Full trays hold up arriving donuts;
   sell them to make room. The fryer holds eighteen donuts at a time.
6. Between shifts, buy upgrades using separate shop coins. Look for effects
   that work together. Unspent coins carry forward.

Clear all six shifts to win. After reaching a target, you may close that shift
early or keep selling to earn more shop coins. Unsold donuts do not count
toward the target when the shift ends.

CONTROLS

All actions work with the mouse. Touch also controls the onscreen buttons;
native mobile packaging and phone-sized interface polish are still planned.

  A / S / D       Cinnamon / powdered / cocoa
  1 / 2 / 3       Destination tray
  Space           Drop six donuts
  Q / W / E       Sell tray 1 / 2 / 3
  Escape          Pause or close a popup
  H               How to play
  M               Mute all sound
  F11             Fullscreen (a browser may handle this shortcut itself)

The pause and settings buttons are available onscreen. Music can be switched
off independently of sound effects.

SAVE & CONTINUE

Active runs and shop purchases save locally. Use Save & return to title or
Save & take a break, then Continue your run. Starting a fresh run asks before
replacing an existing saved run. A lost or completed run ends that save.

The browser stores saves in that browser's local site storage. Clearing site
data removes them, and private browsing may not retain them between sessions.
The Windows save is local to your Windows account. Saves are not synced.

SEND USEFUL PLAYTEST FEEDBACK

On the result screen, Copy playtest report copies your run seed, upgrades,
score, platform, and frame rate. Paste it alongside your feedback. Tell us
what felt good, what was confusing, which upgrade you liked, and how to
repeat any bug. No analytics or personal information is sent by the game.
