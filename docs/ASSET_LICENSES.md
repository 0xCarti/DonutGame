# Asset provenance

## Original project assets

The donut machine, donuts, counter, trays, customers, first-person hands, scoop,
packaging geometry, background props, materials, and animations were authored
for this project. The game uses original procedural geometry, surface textures
and shaders. No models or textures from the reference commercial games are
included. Equipment names in the design discussion are references; the game
uses an original fictional machine design.

Three bitmap illustrations were generated for Golden Hour using OpenAI's built-in
image generation tool on 2026-11-04 UTC: `assets/art/title_keyart.png` (title
illustration), `assets/art/brand_seal.png` (packaging emblem), and
`assets/art/counter_sign.png` (enamel sign artwork). Prompts requested original
art without source-game imagery or machine-brand logos. The prompts and asset
paths are recorded in `docs/ART_PROMPTS.json`, included in the distribution's
LICENSES folder. These are generated illustrations, not photographs of real
equipment. The original outputs are retained separately from the project copies.

The 1.0.3 material pass adds two original base-color textures generated with the
same built-in tool: `assets/materials/counter_wood_color.png` and
`assets/materials/kraft_paper_color.png`. Separate micro-normal and roughness maps
are derived with `tools/bake_material_maps.gd`; these are generated materials,
not scans of third-party objects. Full prompts remain in `ART_PROMPTS.json`.

The curved shaker labels are original vector designs generated by
`tools/generate_shaker_labels.py`, using outlined Fredoka lettering. The six
fairgoer mesh variants in `assets/models/fairgoer_geometry.res` are baked from
original project geometry by `tools/bake_customer_geometry.gd`; they do not
contain downloaded character models. Runtime instancing reuses those meshes
to avoid rebuilding detailed faces during customer arrivals.

The 1.0.5 booth surprises use original procedural geometry and animation for
the service bell, extinguisher, seagull, donut hat, cartoon punch, sneeze,
fryer fire and foam effects. They contain no imported character or effect assets.

The 1.0.6 fire shader and smoke/ember effects are original procedural code in
`scripts/fryer_fire.gd` and `assets/shaders/fryer_fire.gdshader`. New steel, dough, canvas, paper
and ground microdetail maps are original mathematical textures generated by
`tools/generate_surface_detail.py`. Wood and paper normal/roughness maps are
rebuilt at their full source resolution. No third-party fire sprites are used.

All WAV files were synthesized specifically for this project. The original
service/reward effects and 30-second fairground loop are reproducible with
`tools/generate_audio.py`. The finished stand uses fourteen additional assets
from `tools/generate_stand_audio.py`: eleven tactile service and warning cues,
fryer and evening ambience loops, and a 76.8-second original 32-bar evening
theme. Version 1.0.5 adds eight original cues in `assets/audio/gags/`, reproduced
by `tools/generate_gag_audio.py`: `bell.wav`, `bell_reply.wav`, `extinguish.wav`,
`fire.wav`, `hat.wav`, `punch.wav`, `seagull.wav` and `sneeze.wav`. Their manifest
is `assets/audio/gags/manifest.json`.

The generators use mathematical oscillators and deterministic noise;
there are no sampled recordings, downloaded effects or third-party melodies.
Asset manifests record sample lengths and measured peaks. All generators
remain in the source project.

## Fredoka typeface

`assets/fonts/Fredoka.ttf` is the unmodified Fredoka variable font obtained from
the [Google Fonts repository](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/fredoka)
([original font download](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/fredoka/Fredoka%5Bwdth,wght%5D.ttf)).
Its filename was shortened locally; the font contents were not modified.

`assets/fonts/Fredoka-Medium.ttf`, used by the game, is a fixed instance generated
with fontTools from that variable source, with **wght=550** and **wdth=100**.
This generated instance is also distributed under SIL OFL 1.1. The reproducible
fontTools command is documented in `docs/BUILDING.md`.
