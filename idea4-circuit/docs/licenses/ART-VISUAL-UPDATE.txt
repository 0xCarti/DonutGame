Golden Hour: On the Circuit — illustrated interface artwork

All SVG files in assets/ui/ are original vector illustrations created specifically
for this project. No stock photographs, traced commercial art, third-party icon
libraries, logos, or likenesses were used.

The reproducible source is tools/generate_ui_art.py (Python standard library).
It creates 12 venue landscapes, 14 fictional crew portraits, 10 mini-donut recipe
illustrations, 12 equipment illustrations, 7 event scenes, and 30 interface icons.

Icons have white strokes for runtime color tinting. SVG intrinsic dimensions
are 3 times the logical viewBox size for icons and 2 times for illustrations,
providing sharper imported Godot textures on high-density mobile displays.

The illustrations extend this game's existing warm cream, teal, rust, and gold
visual palette. Portraits depict fictional adults. The assets introduce no new
third-party attribution or runtime dependencies.
