GOLDEN HOUR: ON THE CIRCUIT — ASSET PROVENANCE

The fairground/truck illustration (circuit_landscape.svg), donut road app icon
(icon.svg), interface, fictional towns, characters, events, recipes and company
simulation were authored for this demo. The opaque iOS icon is rasterized from
the original SVG. No art, audio, characters, logos or text from BitLife or other
reference games is included. This is an independent project.

Fredoka-Medium.ttf is the existing Golden Hour font instance, derived from the
Fredoka variable typeface using fontTools with wght=550, wdth=100. Copyright
2016 The Fredoka Project Authors; SIL Open Font License 1.1. The full OFL notice
is included at assets/fonts/OFL.txt. Parent-project provenance is documented in
docs/ASSET_LICENSES.md and the reproducible font command in docs/BUILDING.md.

Five original Golden Hour WAV files are reused: click, sale_small, win,
attention, evening_theme. These are synthesized project audio generated by
the parent tools/generate_audio.py and tools/generate_stand_audio.py. They use
mathematical oscillators and deterministic noise, not sampled recordings or
third-party melodies. The music lasts 76.8 seconds and loops optionally.
